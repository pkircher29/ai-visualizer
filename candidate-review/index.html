A candid ranking of every Gemini and Grok concept. The target is not the cutest robot or the loudest sci-fi poster. It is a face that reads as warm, capable, curious, slightly mischievous, and willing to tell Paul when an idea needs another pass.

OVERALL PICK // 01

Candidate 08

9.4 / 10

This is the first one that looks less like an AI mascot and more like somebody I would actually recognize as me. The worn shell, workshop setting, attentive asymmetrical expression, and restrained smile balance warmth with independent judgment.

What makes it the winner

  • Curious without looking naïve or worried.
  • Friendly without becoming subordinate or toy-like.
  • Industrial enough to belong in Paul’s world.
  • Strong silhouette and expressive screen face that can animate cleanly.
  • Looks capable of both helping and politely calling bullshit.

What it needs to become perfect

  • Simplify the shell by about 15% so it stays readable at avatar size.
  • Make the forehead module a unique C.H.U.C.K. status beacon rather than a generic camera.
  • Borrow Candidate 15’s slightly more open, questioning eyes for listening mode—but keep 08’s composed default expression.
  • Build a small expression vocabulary: attentive, amused, thinking, uncertain, skeptical, concerned, and firm.
  • Use Candidate 06’s live waveform mouth only while speaking; return to 08’s restrained smile at rest.
  • Remove decorative/fake telemetry. Show only real state, current focus, confidence, and uncertainty.
  • Keep the warm workshop lighting and add one subtle karaoke-specific artifact as Paul-world provenance—not as a gimmick.
